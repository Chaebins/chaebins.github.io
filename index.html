U1

Learning the Rate but Not the Days: Diagnosing and Repairing Time Series Foundation Models on Zero-Inflated E-Commerce Demand

Knowledge-Based Systems (Elsevier)First author
31,318item-level series released
37–56%zero-demand days
3–20%deficit closed by repair

Summary

  • No evaluated zero-shot TSFM (Chronos-2, Chronos-Bolt, Moirai-2, TimesFM-2.5) beats the classical SBA baseline on category-average MSE.
  • A diagnostic framework attributes the forecast error to four measurable causes.
  • Changing only the fine-tuning objective of a retrieval-augmented TSFM stack closes its 3–20% deficit.
  • The proposed SBA-ZI estimator reaches lower pooled CRPS than every evaluated zero-shot TSFM.

My part

  • Led the study as first author: problem framing, diagnostic framework, experiments and writing.
  • Designed SBA-ZI and built and released the 31,318-series dataset.
