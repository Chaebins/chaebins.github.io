FoodVitals: A Diagnostic Benchmark for LLMs on Nutritional Literacy, Nutrient–Health Relations, and Food Safety

CIKM 2026, Resource TrackSecond author, equal contribution

Summary

  • Public benchmark of 1,158 verified items grounded in USDA, EFSA and FSIS sources.
  • Each item is probed in four formats: multiple choice, yes/no, short answer and ranking.
  • Zero-shot evaluation of six LLMs shows that single-format probes overstate mastery. No model ranks four foods by a nutrient above 29% accuracy.

My part

  • Co-built the benchmark items and their multi-format probes.
  • Ran the evaluation of six LLMs, up to Llama 3.3-70B, with vLLM.

U1

Learning the Rate but Not the Days: Diagnosing and Repairing Time Series Foundation Models on Zero-Inflated E-Commerce Demand

Knowledge-Based SystemsFirst author
31,318item-level series released
37–56%zero-demand days
3–20%deficit closed by repair

Summary

  • No evaluated zero-shot TSFM (Chronos-2, Chronos-Bolt, Moirai-2, TimesFM-2.5) beats the classical SBA baseline on category-average MSE.
  • A diagnostic framework attributes the forecast error to four measurable causes.
  • Changing only the fine-tuning objective of a retrieval-augmented TSFM stack closes its 3–20% deficit.
  • The proposed SBA-ZI estimator reaches lower pooled CRPS than every evaluated zero-shot TSFM.

My part

  • Led the study as first author: problem framing, diagnostic framework, experiments and writing.
  • Designed SBA-ZI and built and released the 31,318-series dataset.

AdaptEat: explainable cross-cuisine food recommendation

KIRD 2026 Global R&D Challengewith New Uzbekistan UniversityJun – Oct 2026
33,001KG nodes, 1,000 recipes
29.2%same-category first dish (24.0% w/o KG)
96.5–97.3%explanations fully verified

Team result

  • A unified Korea–Uzbekistan food knowledge graph (33,001 nodes, 289,036 edges) and a two-route recommender that suggests Korean alternatives to Uzbek dishes.

My part

  • Built the flavour feature from FlavorDB profiles for all 1,000 dishes.
  • Built the KG route (PathSim over shared cooking-step paths), which surfaces candidates beyond the feature-based top 20 for 387 of 498 queries.
  • Fused the KG and feature routes with reciprocal rank fusion: top-3 main-ingredient match 21.4% vs. 4.6% at random.
  • Built the graph-grounded explanation module, its automatic checker and its HTML/CSS view in the public web demo; co-authored the team proposal.

Yanolja joint researchSep 2025 – Aug 2026

My part
